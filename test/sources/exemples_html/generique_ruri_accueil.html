RuriDragon

Slice of LifeSupernatural

Originally appearing as a one-shot in Shueisha's Jump Giga in December 2020, Masaoki Shindo’s RuriDragon (ルリドラゴン) launched into full serialization in Weekly Shōnen Jump in June 2022. Despite the narrative being interrupted by an 18-month hiatus, the series successfully pivoted to the digital version of Weekly Shōnen Jump and the Shōnen Jump+ platform in April 2024, eventually reaching five tankōbon volumes by March 2026.
